Hello,
I'm Hao.

Thanks for checking out my site. I don't get a lot of visitors so it's nice that you're here.

I am currently an electrical engineer at Quartus Engineering. I got my B.S. and M.S. from UC San Diego, where I was a researcher at the Integrated Electronics and Biointerfaces Lab supervised by Dr. Shadi Dayeh.

If I'm not home hanging out with my amazing partner, playing guitar, tending my garden, admiring my tarantulas, or decorating fish tanks — I'm probably somewhere far away with my portable easel, painting nature right in front of me.

Find me on GitHub, view my résumé, or connect on LinkedIn.
